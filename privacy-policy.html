Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 31, 2026

Welcome to HisabMaster ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

Summary: HisabMaster is a personal finance management app that stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal or financial data on external servers.

1. Information We Collect

HisabMaster is designed with your privacy in mind. The app allows you to track:

  • Income and expense transactions
  • Account balances (Cash, Bank, Credit Card, Savings)
  • Budget information
  • Categories and tags for transactions
  • Recurring transaction schedules

Important: All this information is stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to any of your financial data.

2. Data Storage

All data you enter into HisabMaster is stored locally on your device using secure database storage. This means:

  • Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it
  • We cannot access, view, or retrieve your financial information
  • Your data is as secure as your device itself

3. Data Export

The app provides export functionality that allows you to:

  • Export transactions to CSV format
  • Export reports to PDF format

These exports are saved to your device's storage and are under your full control. We do not receive copies of any exported files.

4. Device Permissions

HisabMaster may request the following permissions:

  • Storage Access: To save exported CSV and PDF files to your device
  • Notifications: To send you budget alerts and recurring transaction reminders

These permissions are used solely for the stated purposes and do not involve transmitting data externally.

5. Data Backup

If you use Android's built-in backup feature (Google Backup), your app data may be included in your device backup according to your device settings. This backup is managed by Google and is subject to Google's privacy policy.

6. Third-Party Services

HisabMaster does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not share your data with any third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is intended for general audiences who want to manage their personal finances.

8. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your financial data is protected by your device's security measures (PIN, fingerprint, face unlock)
  • We recommend keeping your device secure and updated
  • Uninstalling the app will delete all stored data

9. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Access: View all your data within the app at any time
  • Export: Export your data using the built-in export feature
  • Delete: Delete individual entries or uninstall the app to remove all data

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with a new "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
